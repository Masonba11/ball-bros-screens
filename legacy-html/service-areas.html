East Valley Solar Screen Service

Arizona's East Valley is one of the fastest-growing regions in the country — and one of the most sun-intense. Ball Bros Screens was built to serve this area specifically. We understand the exposure patterns, the housing styles, and what homeowners in each community need from solar screens.

Whether you're in a newer development in Queen Creek, an established neighborhood in Mesa, or anywhere in between, we provide custom-fit solar screens installed with care and professionalism.

Solar Screens Are Especially Valuable in Arizona

✓

Intense Year-Round Sun

The East Valley receives over 300 days of sunshine per year. Solar screens work hard in every season — not just summer.

High Cooling Costs

Air conditioning is a major expense in Arizona. Reducing solar heat gain through windows may help ease that burden.

✓

Interior Damage from UV

Arizona UV exposure is relentless. Solar screens help protect floors, furniture, and art from premature fading.

Newer Homes with Large Windows

Modern East Valley homes feature large glass areas that look great but can let in a lot of heat — solar screens address this directly.
