Chandler's East and West Exposure Problem — Solved

Chandler has grown into one of the East Valley's most sought-after cities, with a mix of newer master-planned communities and established neighborhoods. Many Chandler homes feature east and west-facing windows that bring in morning light beautifully — but also deliver intense heat at the most inconvenient times of day.

East-facing windows catch the morning sun when rooms haven't yet warmed up, making bedrooms bright and hot early. West-facing windows face the most intense afternoon heat right when you're home from work and trying to relax. Without solar screens, these windows can make your AC work overtime and your rooms uncomfortable.
