Custom Solar Screens for Arizona Homes

What Are Solar Screens — and Why Do Arizona Homeowners Install Them?

Solar screens are exterior window screens engineered to block solar heat and UV radiation before it passes through your window glass. Unlike interior blinds or curtains that trap heat inside your home after it's already entered, exterior solar screens intercept the sun's energy from the outside — where it does the most good.

In the East Valley, where summer temperatures regularly climb above 110°F and intense sun exposure lasts most of the year, solar screens are one of the most practical upgrades a homeowner can make. South- and west-facing windows are particularly vulnerable to intense afternoon sun, and unprotected windows in those positions can make rooms uncomfortably hot and bright.

Ball Bros Screens installs custom solar screens measured and built specifically for your home's windows — not generic off-the-shelf products. The result is a cleaner look, a better fit, and better performance from day one.

Benefits of Solar Screens

What Solar Screens Do for Your Home

Every home in the East Valley deals with intense sun. Here's how solar screens help.

Reduce Heat Gain

Solar screens block a significant portion of solar heat before it reaches your glass, helping to reduce the temperature of sun-exposed rooms.

🌿

UV Protection

High-quality solar screen material filters out harmful UV rays that cause fading in flooring, furniture, artwork, and other interior surfaces.

Glare Reduction

Harsh glare on TVs, computer screens, and throughout rooms is dramatically reduced — without requiring curtains or blackout shades.

Daytime Privacy

From outside your home, solar screens make it difficult to see in during daylight hours — while you maintain a clear view from inside.

Lighter AC Load

By reducing solar heat gain through windows, solar screens can help your air conditioning system work less hard to maintain your home's temperature.

Enhanced Curb Appeal

Modern solar screens have a clean, uniform appearance that adds a finished, polished look to your home's exterior.

Solar Screen Installation Questions

Solar screens are exterior window screens made from a specially woven material designed to block solar heat and UV rays. They're installed on the outside of your windows — just like regular insect screens — but instead of just keeping bugs out, they actively reduce heat and sun exposure entering your home.
Custom-fit screens ensure there are no gaps at the edges that would allow heat or light to leak through. They also look much cleaner on your home — a screen that's slightly too large or too small is visible and can look sloppy. Ball Bros Screens measures each window individually so every screen fits precisely.
The percentage refers to how much of the solar energy the screen blocks. A 90% screen blocks approximately 90% of solar heat and UV, making it better for maximum heat reduction and privacy. An 80% screen provides good heat reduction while allowing slightly more light and visibility. We'll help you choose based on your priorities for each window or room.
Most residential installations are completed in one visit. The duration depends on the number of windows being screened and the size of your home. We work efficiently and clean up completely before we leave.
